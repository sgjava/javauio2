sudo nano /boot/armbianEnv.txt

Remove spi-spidev from overlays=
Remove param_spidev_spi_bus=0

Append your custom overlay:

user_overlays=pine64-dual-spi

Compile and apply the overlay:

sudo dtc -I dts -O dtb -o /boot/overlay-user/pine64-dual-spi.dtbo pine64-dual-spi.dts

Reboot the board:

sudo reboot

Check for active SPI devices and kernel output after boot:

ls -l /dev/spidev*
dmesg | grep -i spi
